## Meselson and Stahl's experiment proved :

---

#### Matthew Meselson and Franklin Stahl (1958) used heavy nitrogen isotope ($^{15}\text{N}$) density gradient centrifugation in *E. coli* to definitively prove that DNA replicates by the semiconservative mechanism.
