## The first codon to be deciphered was ________ which codes for ________ :

---

#### Marshall Nirenberg and Heinrich Matthaei synthesized a homopolymer poly-U (polyuridylic acid) in 1961 and deciphered that the triplet codon UUU codes for the amino acid phenylalanine.
