## mRNA molecule is produced by :

---

#### Transcription is the biological process in which genetic information from the template strand of DNA is copied into a complementary messenger RNA (mRNA) molecule by RNA polymerase. Replication produces DNA from DNA, while translation synthesizes polypeptides from mRNA.
