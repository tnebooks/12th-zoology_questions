## Which of the following is the correct sequence of event with reference to the central dogma ?

---

#### The central dogma of molecular biology proposed by Francis Crick states that genetic information flows sequentially from DNA to DNA (Replication), from DNA to mRNA (Transcription), and from mRNA to Protein (Translation).
