## Hershey and Chase's experiment with bacteriophage showed that :

---

#### Alfred Hershey and Martha Chase (1952) used $^{32}\text{P}$-labeled DNA and $^{35}\text{S}$-labeled protein coats in T2 bacteriophage infecting *E. coli* to prove conclusively that DNA, not protein, enters the host cell and acts as the genetic material.
