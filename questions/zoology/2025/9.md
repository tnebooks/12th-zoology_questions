The figure given below is a diagrammatic representation of response of organisms to abiotic factors. What do A, B and C represent respectively ?
![](..\2025q9.png)

---
In the organismic response graph, curve A shows internal levels changing directly with the external environment (Conformer), line B shows a constant internal environment (Regulator), and curve C shows regulation up to a limit before conforming (Partial Regulator).
