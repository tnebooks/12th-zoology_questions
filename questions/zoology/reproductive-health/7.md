## A contraceptive pill prevents ovulation by :

---

#### Steroidal oral contraceptive pills exert negative feedback on the hypothalamus and anterior pituitary gland, thereby inhibiting gonadotropins (FSH and LH). Without the mid-cycle LH surge, ovulation cannot occur.
