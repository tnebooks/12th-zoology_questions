## Match the following:\n(1) Natural method - (iv) Periodic Abstinence\n(2) Barrier method - (iii) Condom\n(3) Surgical method - (ii) Vasectomy\n(4) Intra Uterine Device - (i) Cu-T

---

#### Periodic abstinence is a rhythm (natural) method of contraception. Condoms provide a mechanical barrier to prevent sperm entry. Vasectomy is surgical male sterilization. Cu-T (Copper-T) is a copper-releasing intrauterine contraceptive device.
