#### Diaphragms, cervical caps, and vaults are physical/mechanical barrier contraceptives made of rubber that block sperm entry through the cervix and are reusable. They are mechanical barriers, not chemical barriers (which include spermicidal foams, jellies, and creams). Thus, Statement 1 is correct and Statement 2 is false.
