## The permanent birth control method in males is :

---

#### Vasectomy is a surgical sterilization method in males where a small portion of both vasa deferentia is cut and tied off to prevent sperm from entering the semen. Tubectomy is female sterilization, appendectomy is the surgical removal of the appendix, and oral contraceptives are temporary hormonal methods.
