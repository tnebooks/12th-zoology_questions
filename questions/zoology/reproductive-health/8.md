## Which one of the following groups includes sexually transmitted diseases caused by bacteria only ?

---

#### Syphilis (*Treponema pallidum*), chlamydiasis (*Chlamydia trachomatis*), and gonorrhoea (*Neisseria gonorrhoeae*) are all bacterial STIs. Trichomoniasis is protozoan, candidiasis is fungal, and pediculosis is an ectoparasitic infestation.
