## Statement I : Castration (or) surgical removal of testis is known as Orchidectomy.\nStatement II : It leads to difficulty in urination.

---

#### Castration or surgical excision of one or both testes is medically termed orchidectomy. Because the urethra is part of the urinary tract and not removed during orchidectomy, it does not impede urination. Hence, Statement I is correct and Statement II is incorrect.
