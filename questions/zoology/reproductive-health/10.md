## Select the incorrect action of hormonal contraceptive pills from the following :

---

#### Hormonal contraceptive pills are administered to females; they work by preventing ovulation, thickening cervical mucus to block sperm transport, and making the endometrium hostile to implantation. They do not act on males or inhibit spermatogenesis.
