## In embryo transfer technique, ________ celled blastomere is transferred into the uterus.

---

#### In IVF-ET (In Vitro Fertilization and Embryo Transfer), embryos with up to 8 blastomeres are transferred into the fallopian tube (ZIFT), whereas embryos with more than 8 blastomeres are transferred directly into the uterus (IUT - Intra Uterine Transfer).
