## In a lake, the water is cold and clear, supporting little life. This stage is called as :

---

#### An oligotrophic lake is characterized by cold, clear water with low nutrient levels (such as nitrates and phosphates), high dissolved oxygen, and minimal organic matter, thus supporting relatively little biological life. Eutrophic and accelerated eutrophic lakes, in contrast, have excessive nutrients and dense algal/plant blooms.
