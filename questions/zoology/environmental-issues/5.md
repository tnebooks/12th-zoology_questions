## The highest DDT concentration in aquatic food chain shall occur in :

---

#### Through biological magnification, fat-soluble and non-biodegradable pesticides like DDT concentrate at progressively higher trophic levels, reaching their maximum concentration in top predators such as fish-eating birds.
