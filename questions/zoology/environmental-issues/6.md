## Without altering water quality, ________ is/are an ideal disinfectant used in waste water treatment.

---

#### Ultraviolet (UV) radiation inactivates microbial pathogens by disrupting their nucleic acids without adding chemicals or altering the taste, odor, or chemical properties of water.
