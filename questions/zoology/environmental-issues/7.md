## The 'thickness' of Stratospheric Ozone layer is measured in :

---

#### The thickness of stratospheric ozone from the ground to the top of the atmosphere is measured in Dobson Units (DU), where 1 DU equals an ozone layer of 0.01 mm thickness at standard temperature and pressure.
