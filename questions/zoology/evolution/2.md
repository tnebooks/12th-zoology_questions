## Which of the following was the contribution of Hugo de Vries ?

---

#### Hugo de Vries proposed the Mutation Theory of evolution in 1901 based on his breeding experiments with the evening primrose (*Oenothera lamarckiana*), stating that new species arise through sudden, large, discontinuous variations. In contrast, the theory of inheritance of acquired characters was proposed by Lamarck, the germplasm theory by August Weismann, and the theory of natural selection by Charles Darwin.
