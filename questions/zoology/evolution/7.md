## A population will not exist in Hardy Weinberg equilibrium, if :

---

#### Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium relies on random mating, large population size, and the absence of mutations, gene flow (migration), and natural selection. Non-random or selective mating alters allele frequencies, disturbing equilibrium.
