## The term 'Biogenesis' was coined by :

---

#### The term 'Biogenesis' was originally introduced by English physician Henry Charlton Bastian to describe the generation of living organisms. Thomas Henry Huxley later clarified and popularized biogenesis as life originating only from pre-existing life.
