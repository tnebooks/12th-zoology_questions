## Who proposed the 'Germplasm theory' ?

---

#### August Weismann disproved Lamarck's theory of inheritance of acquired characters by cutting off the tails of mice over 22 successive generations and proposed the Germplasm theory, stating that only variations occurring in the germplasm are inheritable.
