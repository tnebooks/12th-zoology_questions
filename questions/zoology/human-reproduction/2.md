## The mature sperms are stored in the :

---

#### Spermatids are produced in the seminiferous tubules of the testes, but the resulting spermatozoa travel to the epididymis, where they undergo physiological maturation, gain motility and fertilizing capacity, and are temporarily stored until ejaculation.
