## The Androgen Binding Protein (ABP) is produced by :

---

#### Sertoli cells (nurse cells) in the seminiferous tubules secrete Androgen Binding Protein (ABP) under the stimulation of FSH. ABP concentrates testosterone within the seminiferous tubules to ensure optimal spermatogenesis. Leydig cells secrete testosterone under LH stimulation.
