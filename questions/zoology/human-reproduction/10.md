## The ovary remains attached with pelvic wall and the uterus by an ovarian ligament, called :

---

#### Each ovary is held in position in the pelvic cavity by peritoneal folds and ligaments, notably the mesovarium (a fold of peritoneum holding it to the broad ligament) and the suspensory and ovarian ligaments.
