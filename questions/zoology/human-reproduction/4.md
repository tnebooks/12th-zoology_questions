## Assertion (A) : Head of the sperm consists of Acrosome and Mitochondria.\nReason (R) : Acrosome contains spiral rows of Mitochondria.

---

#### The head of a human sperm contains only the condensed haploid nucleus and the acrosome cap containing proteolytic enzymes. Mitochondria are spirally arranged (nebenkern) exclusively in the middle piece to provide energy for motility. Therefore, both assertion and reason are factually incorrect.
