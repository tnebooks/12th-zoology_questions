## The most important hormone in initiating and maintaining lactation after birth is :

---

#### Prolactin, secreted by the anterior pituitary, stimulates the mammary alveolar epithelium to synthesize and secrete milk. Oxytocin acts as the milk ejection ('let-down') hormone by contracting myoepithelial cells.
