## The process which the sperm undergoes before penetrating the ovum is :

---

#### Capacitation is the physiological conditioning that spermatozoa undergo within the female reproductive tract (lasting about 5-6 hours), during which glycoprotein coats and cholesterol are destabilized from the acrosome, enabling the acrosome reaction and fertilization of the secondary oocyte.
