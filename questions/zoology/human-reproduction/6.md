## Which is the correct order of action ?

---

#### Luteinizing Hormone (LH) from the anterior pituitary acts directly on the Leydig (interstitial) cells of the testes to stimulate secretion of testosterone, which in turn acts on the germinal epithelium to drive spermatogenesis.
