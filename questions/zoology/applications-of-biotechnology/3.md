## PCR proceeds in three distinct steps governed by temperature, they are in order of :

---

#### Each PCR cycle consists of: (1) Denaturation at $94-96^\circ\text{C}$ to separate dsDNA, (2) Primer Annealing at $50-65^\circ\text{C}$, and (3) Extension/Synthesis at $72^\circ\text{C}$ by *Taq* DNA polymerase.
