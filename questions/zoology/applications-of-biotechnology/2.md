## ELISA is mainly used for :

---

#### ELISA (Enzyme-Linked Immunosorbent Assay) is a widely used biochemical diagnostic technique based on antigen-antibody reactions. It is primarily utilized for the rapid detection of pathogens (such as viruses, bacteria, and parasites) or the antibodies generated against them in biological samples.
