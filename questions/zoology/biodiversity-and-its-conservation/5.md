## According to Alexander Von Humboldt, species - area relationship is $\log S = \log C + Z \log A$, where 'Z' is :

---

#### In Alexander von Humboldt's logarithmic species-area relationship equation $\log S = \log C + Z \log A$, $S$ is species richness, $A$ is area, $C$ is the Y-intercept, and $Z$ represents the regression coefficient or slope of the line.
