## Assertion: The environmental conditions of the tropics are favourable for speciation and diversity of organisms.\nReason: The climate seasons, temperature, humidity and photoperiod are more or less stable and congenial.

---

#### Tropical regions have remained relatively undisturbed for millions of years with relatively constant climate and abundant solar energy, promoting niche specialization and high speciation rates. Thus, both statements are true and related.
