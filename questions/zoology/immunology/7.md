## Which of the following groups of diseases have been controlled by the use of vaccines and by creating awareness among the people ?

---

#### Widespread immunization programs have completely eradicated smallpox and effectively controlled poliomyelitis, diphtheria, and tetanus. Vaccines do not currently exist for amoebiasis, AIDS, or the common cold.
