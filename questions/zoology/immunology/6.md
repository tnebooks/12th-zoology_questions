## A person is injured and got swelling. The swelling, due to the infection of tissue is an example of :

---

#### Tissue injury and localized infection trigger an inflammatory barrier response mediated by histamine and prostaglandins, increasing capillary permeability and blood flow to cause swelling (edema), redness, and heat.
