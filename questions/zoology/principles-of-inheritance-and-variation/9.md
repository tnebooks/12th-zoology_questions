## When a *Drosophila* body cells contain 3 sets of autosomes and 2 X chromosomes, what will be the sex of the fly ?

---

#### In *Drosophila*, sex index ratio $X:A = 2 / 3 = 0.67$. Ratios falling strictly between 0.5 (male) and 1.0 (female) develop into intersex individuals displaying both male and female characteristics.
