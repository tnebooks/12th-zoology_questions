## Down's Syndrome is a genetic disorder which is caused by the presence of an extra chromosome number :

---

#### Down's syndrome is an autosomal aneuploid condition caused by trisomy of chromosome 21 ($47, XX, +21$ or $47, XY, +21$), first described by Langdon Down in 1866.
