## Which of the following phenotypes is not possible in the progeny of the parental genotypic combination $I^A I^O \times I^A I^B$ ?

---

#### A cross between $I^A I^O$ (blood group A) and $I^A I^B$ (blood group AB) yields progeny genotypes $I^A I^A$ (Type A), $I^A I^B$ (Type AB), $I^A I^O$ (Type A), and $I^B I^O$ (Type B). Because blood group O requires homozygous recessive alleles ($I^O I^O$), it cannot be produced.
