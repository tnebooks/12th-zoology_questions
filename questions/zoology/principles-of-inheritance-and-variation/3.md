## Patau's Syndrome is also referred to as :

---

#### Patau's syndrome is caused by the presence of an extra copy of chromosome 13 (Trisomy 13) due to meiotic nondisjunction. Trisomy 18 is Edwards syndrome, Trisomy 21 is Down syndrome, and 45,XO is Turner syndrome.
