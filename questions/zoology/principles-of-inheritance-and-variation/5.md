## Which of the following rule is used to calculate the number of Barr bodies in a cell ?

---

#### According to the Lyon hypothesis and the N-1 rule, the number of Barr bodies (inactivated X chromosomes) in a somatic cell is equal to the total number of X chromosomes ($N$) minus one ($N-1$). Normal females ($XX$) have 1 Barr body, while normal males ($XY$) have 0.
