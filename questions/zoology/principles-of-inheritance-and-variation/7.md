## XO type of sex determination and XY type of sex determination are examples of :

---

#### In male heterogamety, the male produces two different types of gametes (either $X$ and $Y$ as in humans and Drosophila, or $X$ and $O$ as in grasshoppers), while the female produces only one type of gamete ($X$).
