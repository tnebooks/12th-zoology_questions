## Which of the following phenotypes in the Progeny are possible from the parental combination $A \times B$ ?

---

#### When both parents are heterozygous for ABO blood groups ($I^A I^O \times I^B I^O$), their progeny can inherit any of the four combinations: $I^A I^B$ (group AB), $I^A I^O$ (group A), $I^B I^O$ (group B), or $I^O I^O$ (group O).
