## What is the sex of *Drosophila* which possess 2A + XXY chromosomes ?

---

#### According to Bridges' genic balance system in *Drosophila*, sex is determined by the ratio of X chromosomes to sets of autosomes ($X:A$). Here, the ratio is $2X / 2A = 1.0$, which specifies a fertile diploid female. The Y chromosome has no role in determining maleness in *Drosophila*.
