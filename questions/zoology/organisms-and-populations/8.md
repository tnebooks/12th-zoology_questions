## Which of the following is an r-species ?

---

#### $r$-selected species are opportunistic organisms that mature quickly, have small body sizes, reproduce early, and produce large numbers of small offspring (e.g., insects, bacteria). Rhinoceros, humans, and whales are $K$-selected.
