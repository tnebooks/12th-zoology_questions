## Organisms which can survive a wide range of temperature are called :

---

#### Eurytherms are organisms that can tolerate and thrive in a wide range of environmental temperatures. Organisms restricted to a narrow range of temperatures are called stenotherms, while endotherms and ectotherms refer to how organisms regulate body temperature.
