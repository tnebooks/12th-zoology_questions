## Animals that can move from freshwater to seawater is called as :

---

#### Catadromous fish (e.g., *Anguilla*) spend the major portion of their lives feeding in freshwater and migrate downstream to the ocean/sea to spawn. Anadromous fish migrate from sea to freshwater.
