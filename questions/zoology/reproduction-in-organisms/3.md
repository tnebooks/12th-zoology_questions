## Paedogenetic parthenogenesis occurs in :

---

#### Paedogenetic parthenogenesis is a phenomenon where larvae produce a new generation of larvae by parthenogenesis, occurring characteristically in the larvae of the gall fly (*Miastor*) and liver fluke. In honeybees and *Solenobia*, parthenogenesis occurs in adult stages.
