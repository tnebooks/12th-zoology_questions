## In which type of Parthenogenesis are only males produced ?

---

#### In arrhenotoky, unfertilized eggs develop exclusively into males, as seen in honeybees and wasps. In thelytoky, only females are produced (e.g., *Lacerta saxicola armeniaca*), and in amphitoky, unfertilized eggs can develop into either males or females (e.g., *Aphis*).
