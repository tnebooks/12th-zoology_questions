## In which mode of reproduction, variations are seen ?

---

#### Sexual reproduction involves gamete formation via meiosis and crossing over, as well as the random fusion of gametes, leading to genetic recombination and distinct variations in the offspring. Asexual reproduction and parthenogenesis yield offspring genetically identical to the parent (clones).
