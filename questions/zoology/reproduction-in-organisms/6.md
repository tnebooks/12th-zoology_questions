## The mode of reproduction in bacteria is by :

---

#### In bacteria, primitive sexual reproduction occurs by genetic recombination through conjugation, transformation, and transduction. Conjugation involves direct contact between donor and recipient cells via a conjugation tube to transfer genetic material. Endospore formation is a perennation mechanism during unfavorable conditions, not reproduction.
