## Assertion (A) : In nature many organisms produce clones through asexual reproduction.\nReason (R) : By this process, genetically identical individuals are produced.

---

#### Asexual reproduction involves only a single parent and mitotic cell divisions without gametic fusion. Consequently, the offspring are morphologically and genetically identical to each other and to their parent, qualifying them as clones. Thus, both assertion and reason are true, and the reason correctly explains the assertion.
