## If buds are formed on the outer surface of the parent body, it is called as :

---

#### When buds develop on the outer surface of the parental body, as seen in *Hydra*, the process is termed exogenous budding. In contrast, endogenous budding (such as gemmules in sponges) occurs inside the parental body, and fragmentation is the breaking of the parent into distinct fragments.
