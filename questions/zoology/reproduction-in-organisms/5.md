## Parthenogenesis was first discovered by :

---

#### Parthenogenesis was first discovered in aphids by the Swiss naturalist Charles Bonnet in 1745. Barr and Bertram discovered sex chromatin (Barr body), and Abraham Trembley studied regeneration in *Hydra*.
