## The sporozoites of *Plasmodium vivax* are formed from :

---

#### In the gut of the female *Anopheles* mosquito, the zygote forms an ookinete that penetrates the stomach wall to develop into an oocyst. The oocyst undergoes multiple fission (sporogony) to produce infective spindle-shaped sporozoites.
