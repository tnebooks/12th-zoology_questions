## Match the pathogens with respective diseases caused by them and select the correct match :\n(A) *Leishmania donovani* - (ii) Kala-azar\n(B) *Wuchereria bancrofti* - (iv) Filariasis\n(C) *Trypanosoma gambiense* - (iii) Sleeping sickness\n(D) *Entamoeba histolytica* - (i) Amoebiasis

---

#### *Leishmania donovani* causes Kala-azar (visceral leishmaniasis), *Wuchereria bancrofti* causes lymphatic filariasis, *Trypanosoma gambiense* causes African sleeping sickness, and *Entamoeba histolytica* causes amoebic dysentery.
