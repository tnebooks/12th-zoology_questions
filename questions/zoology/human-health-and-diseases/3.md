## Exo-erythrocytic schizogony of *Plasmodium* takes place in :

---

#### When an infected female *Anopheles* mosquito injects sporozoites into the human bloodstream, they first enter hepatic parenchymal (liver) cells where they undergo asexual multiplication (exo-erythrocytic schizogony) before invading erythrocytes.
