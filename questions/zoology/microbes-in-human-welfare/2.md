## The primary treatment of waste water treatment is :

---

#### Primary treatment in sewage treatment involves the physical removal of both large and small particulate solids through sequential filtration and sedimentation. Biological treatment (aeration, sludge digestion) occurs during secondary treatment, and chemical disinfection occurs during tertiary treatment.
