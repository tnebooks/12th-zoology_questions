Match the following. (1) Natural method (i) Cu-T (2) Barrier method (ii) Vasectomy (3) Surgical method (iii) Condom (4) Intra Uterine Device (iv) Periodic Abstinence

---
Periodic abstinence is a natural contraceptive method (1-iv), condoms act as a barrier method (2-iii), vasectomy is a surgical method (3-ii), and Copper-T (Cu-T) is an intra-uterine device (4-i).
