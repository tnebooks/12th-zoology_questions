Which of the following phenotypes in the Progeny are possible from the parental combination A × B ?

---
When heterozygous parents with blood groups A (IA IO) and B (IB IO) cross, the possible progeny genotypes are IA IB (AB), IA IO (A), IB IO (B), and IO IO (O), allowing all four blood group phenotypes.
