Select the incorrect action of hormonal contraceptive pills from the following.

---
Hormonal contraceptive pills are taken by females to prevent pregnancy by inhibiting ovulation, thickening cervical mucus to block sperm transport, and altering the uterine endometrium; they do not inhibit spermatogenesis.
