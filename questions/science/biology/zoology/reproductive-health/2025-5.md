Which one of the following groups includes sexually transmitted diseases caused by bacteria only ?

---
Syphilis (Treponema pallidum), chlamydiasis (Chlamydia trachomatis), and gonorrhoea (Neisseria gonorrhoeae) are all bacterial sexually transmitted infections, whereas trichomoniasis is protozoan, candidiasis is fungal, and pediculosis is caused by lice.
