When a Drosophila body cells contain 3 sets of autosomes and 2 X chromosomes, what will be the sex of the fly ?

---
In Drosophila, sex is determined by the ratio of X chromosomes to sets of autosomes (X:A). With 2 X chromosomes and 3 sets of autosomes, the ratio is 2:3 (0.67), which produces an intersex fly.
