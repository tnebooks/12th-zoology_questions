Which of the following phenotypes is not possible in the progeny of the parental genotypic combination Iᴬ Iᴼ × Iᴬ Iᴮ ?

---
A cross between parental genotypes IA IO and IA IB yields progeny with genotypes IA IA (A), IA IB (AB), IA IO (A), and IB IO (B). Because the IA IB parent lacks the IO allele, blood group O is not possible.
