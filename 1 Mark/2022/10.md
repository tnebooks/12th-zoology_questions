  - "LH → Leydig cells → Testosterone → Spermatogenesis"

Which is the correct order of action ?

---
Luteinizing hormone (LH) stimulates Leydig (interstitial) cells in the testes to secrete testosterone, which in turn stimulates spermatogenesis.
