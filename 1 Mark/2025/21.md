Meselson and Stahl’s experiment proved __________.

---
Matthew Meselson and Franklin Stahl used heavy nitrogen (15N) in E. coli to prove that DNA replication is semi-conservative, meaning each daughter DNA molecule retains one parental strand and one newly synthesized strand.
