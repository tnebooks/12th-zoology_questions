Assertion (A) : Head of the sperm consists of Acrosome and Mitochondria. Reason (R) : Acrosome contains spiral rows of Mitochondria.

---
Both Assertion and Reason are false because the sperm head contains only the haploid nucleus and the acrosome, whereas spiral rows of mitochondria are located in the middle piece of the sperm.
