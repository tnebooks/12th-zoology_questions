Which of the following rule is used to calculate the number of Barr bodies in a cell ?

---
According to the N - 1 rule (Lyon's hypothesis), the number of Barr bodies (inactivated X chromosomes) in a diploid somatic cell is one less than the total number of X chromosomes (N).
